# Nodeblix design strategy

## What to learn from the references

- **Thoughtworks:** Show expertise in writing and architecture, do not claim it in adjectives. Editorial insights support commercial pages.
- **Accenture:** Keep a wide service and industry map without turning the header into a maze. Breadth is an information-architecture problem.
- **IBM Consulting:** Talk about modernization as integration with what already exists. Partner and platform names appear only when they are real.
- **Slalom:** Keep the voice human. Business value is explained as a constraint, not a slogan.
- **Snowflake:** Explain data movement visually. Use cases beat feature lists.
- **Vercel:** Precise type, restrained colour, one primary action. Hierarchy does the work that decoration usually tries to do.
- **AWS:** Different readers need different depth. Service pages go deep; the homepage stays navigable.
- **Instrument / Pentagram:** Art direction is a system — photography, type, and empty space — not a new effect on every section.

## Conventions to avoid

- Three identical cards under a giant centered headline.
- Purple-blue gradients, glow borders, glassmorphism, particles.
- Invented metrics, logos, testimonials, or employee portraits.
- The same section template repeated down every page.
- Keyword-stuffed H1s that all follow “X services for Y”.

## How Nodeblix differentiates

Engineering judgment is visible: microservices are not the default, CRM platforms are not named as partnerships until verified, and work examples are labeled illustrative. The visual system is a light editorial surface with navy used for method and architecture, not for every band.

## Balancing design, credibility, SEO, and leads

- One commercial URL per service; insights target informational queries and link back.
- Original photography and HTML/SVG diagrams instead of stock or illegible generated UI.
- A single primary conversion path: Discuss a project. Contextual secondary links (approach, services, insights) replace a button after every paragraph.
- Honest gaps (no public mailbox, no case-study metrics) stay visible so the site can be trusted after launch.
